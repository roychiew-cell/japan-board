LEGOLAND Japan

Family theme park with LEGO attractions and toddler-friendly play areas.

Route: B/C-B. Isewangan, Meiko-Chuo IC; use Kinjo-futo municipal parking

Extra driving: B/C-B: +10 min / +3 km Large sightseeing diversion. Driving excludes the on-site allowance, parking queues and child-seat handling. Recheck traffic and ramps in navigation on the travel day.
B/C-B: Meiko-Chuo IC → Kinjo-futo municipal parking → onward Nagoya urban approach. C-A/C-B share the corresponding A/B roads after Yokkaichi JCT; the unchanged prefix cancels in this comparison. Select the exact continuation before using its number.

Time to allow: 240–360 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: A main day outing, especially with the twins. Early departure from VISON is needed; 15:00 hotel arrival is unlikely with a full visit. Mostly outdoors; indoor play, attractions and dining available. Substantial walking from parking and around the park; stroller useful and ride height limits apply.

Toilets / baby facilities: Park toilets and restrooms at Duplo Baby Care. Duplo Baby Care has nursing, changing and warm-water facilities; additional nursing rooms available.

Hours (Japan time): 19 December 2026 hours not verified from the dynamic operator calendar; do not assume a generic 10:00 opening. Closed: Variable operating calendar; check target date and ride closures before buying. Exact travel-date operation unverified.

Food budget: Planning budget: park meal JPY 1,200–2,500 (≈ SGD 9.72–20.25 · ≈ MYR 30.84–64.25); unverified, plus admission. Admission / parking: Date-dependent tickets and municipal parking extra; target-date quote not verified.

Ghibli Park

Reserved-entry Ghibli exhibition areas spread through a large landscaped park.

Route: B/C-B. East of Nagoya in Expo 2005 Aichi Commemorative Park; Nagakute IC / Nagoya-Seto road approach

Extra driving: B/C-B: +45 min / +42 km Large sightseeing diversion. Driving excludes the on-site allowance, parking queues and child-seat handling. Recheck traffic and ramps in navigation on the travel day.
B/C-B: Nagoya Daini-Kanjo / Nagoya-Seto road, Nagakute IC → Expo park north parking → Nagoya Station. C-A/C-B share the corresponding A/B roads after Yokkaichi JCT; the unchanged prefix cancels in this comparison. Select the exact continuation before using its number.

Time to allow: 240–360 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: Advance timed ticket required; treat as the day’s destination. Long outdoor walks are demanding for grandparents in December. Mixed indoor exhibitions and substantial outdoor travel between areas. Large distances. Strollers may be used in the surrounding park but must be parked outside some exhibition facilities.

Toilets / baby facilities: Accessible toilets and changing areas listed across the park areas. Nursing/baby rooms in Grand Warehouse and Valley of Witches.

Hours (Japan time): Published weekend hours 09:00–17:00; December calendar exists but 19 December colour-coded closure state not independently resolved. Closed: Tuesdays (next weekday if holiday), year-end and designated maintenance days. Exact travel-date operation unverified. Do not combine a full Ghibli visit with a full LEGOLAND day. The operator announced December-ticket sales on 10 October 2026.

Food budget: Planning budget: meals JPY 1,000–2,000 (≈ SGD 8.10–16.20 · ≈ MYR 25.70–51.40); unverified, plus reserved tickets. Admission / parking: Reservation-only, ticket types/prices vary. Three-year-olds and younger free under current rules; availability for 19 December not checked.

Motoring-themed amusement park with attractions for younger children.

Port of Nagoya Public Aquarium

Large aquarium with marine exhibits and dolphin programmes.

Route: B/C-B. Nagoya Port/Garden Pier; approach from Isewangan via Nagoya urban roads

Extra driving: B/C-B: +15 min / -1 km Large sightseeing diversion. Driving excludes the on-site allowance, parking queues and child-seat handling. Recheck traffic and ramps in navigation on the travel day.
B/C-B: Meiko-Chuo IC → Garden Pier visitor parking → Nagoya Expressway Route 4 onward approach. C-A/C-B share the corresponding A/B roads after Yokkaichi JCT; the unchanged prefix cancels in this comparison. Select the exact continuation before using its number.

Time to allow: 120–180 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: Good wet-weather option with the twins; a shorter exhibit visit is easier on grandparents. Large indoor exhibit areas; dolphin viewing includes an exposed stadium. Considerable walking between buildings; seated shows offer a rest but may be cold.

Toilets / baby facilities: Toilets available; detailed accessibility not verified in this pass. Nursing/changing-room locations unverified in this pass.

Hours (Japan time): Winter (December–mid-March) 09:30–17:00; last admission 16:00. Closed: Mondays (next day if holiday), with holiday-season exceptions and maintenance closures. Exact travel-date operation unverified.

Food budget: Planning budget: lunch/snacks JPY 1,000–2,000 (≈ SGD 8.10–16.20 · ≈ MYR 25.70–51.40); unverified. Admission / parking: Admission and port parking extra; current ticket quote not verified.

Kakegawa Kachoen bird and flower park

Large temperature-controlled flower glasshouse with birds, shows and a children’s corner.

Route: T/S. Tomei Kakegawa IC; Shin-Tomei uses a different, longer local approach

Extra driving: S: +45 min / +23 km
T: +10 min / +4 km Driving excludes the on-site allowance, parking queues and child-seat handling. Recheck traffic and ramps in navigation on the travel day.
S: Mori-Kakegawa IC → Kachoen → Tomei Kakegawa IC → Yaizu IC / local connector → Shin-Tomei Shizuoka SA anchor (corridor change included). The observed S approach uses Mori-Kakegawa outward, then Tomei Kakegawa / Yaizu and the Shizuoka SA connection onward; it is not a measured return to Mori-Kakegawa IC. Corridor switching is included in the whole-route comparison.
T: Kakegawa IC → Kachoen → Kakegawa IC eastbound.

Time to allow: 90–150 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: One of the strongest December options for toddlers and grandparents; allow handwashing after bird contact. Mostly indoor glasshouse, with optional outdoor ponds and shows. Operator describes barrier-free access suitable for strollers and wheelchairs.

Toilets / baby facilities: Toilets and wheelchair-friendly facilities described in operator material. Operator confirms nursing room, changing provision and hot water for milk.

Hours (Japan time): Current municipal tourism page: Sunday/holidays 09:00–17:00, last entry 16:30; weekdays 09:00–16:30. Exact December operator calendar still unverified. Closed: Brochure: second/fourth Thursdays, with holiday/peak-season exceptions; check current calendar. Exact travel-date operation unverified. Municipal Sunday schedule replaces the older general brochure closing time; exact 20 December operation remains provisional. Operator information page confirms current admission and family facilities.

Food budget: Planning budget: café/meal JPY 800–1,500 (≈ SGD 6.48–12.15 · ≈ MYR 20.56–38.55); unverified. Admission / parking: Current operator listing: adult JPY 2,000 (≈ SGD 16.20 · ≈ MYR 51.40); 65+ JPY 1,700 (≈ SGD 13.77 · ≈ MYR 43.69) with age proof; preschool children free.

Indoor museum of Shinkansen, historic trains and maglev technology.

Time to allow: 90–150 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.
