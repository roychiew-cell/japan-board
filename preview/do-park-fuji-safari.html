Fuji Safari Park

Fuji Safari Park (near Mt Fuji). Drive through in the hire car: no charge beyond admission, as many laps as you like, 50 to 60 minutes a lap. Open daily; entry 10:00 to 15:30 on 17 to 23 Dec. Adult JPY 3,200 (≈ SGD 25.92 · ≈ MYR 82.24), online JPY 2,700 (≈ SGD 21.87 · ≈ MYR 69.39), 65+ JPY 2,500 (≈ SGD 20.25 · ≈ MYR 64.25) (bring photo ID), age 3 and under free: JPY 14,600 (≈ SGD 118.26 · ≈ MYR 375.22) at the gate for 3 adults and 2 seniors. Possible days are Mon 21 or Tue 22 Dec from the Lake Yamanaka house; allow 45 to 60 minutes each way in winter. Children under 3 cannot enter the Dog House (2 and under not admitted) and the Cat House is for age 3 and over; strollers are not allowed on the Jungle Bus. Heated rocks for the lions and heated barns for the giraffes are NOT confirmed: the park says only that giraffe, cheetah and sitatunga may not be visible in rain, snow or cold. The Walking Safari is listed only until November. Before relying on it: ask for snow chains by Sat 12 Dec and settle the child seats (none are booked, and the Alphard may not carry all seven with two seats fitted).
