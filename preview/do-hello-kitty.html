A Sanrio shop inside the Gotemba outlet mall, selling Hello Kitty and other Sanrio goods from small fashion items to stationery and kids' items.

Official site ↗

Place, children, costs, booking and what is not confirmed

WhereGotemba Premium Outlets, Gotemba, Shizuoka (shop unit 3855)
Address: Gotemba Premium Outlets, shop unit 3855, 1312 Fukasawa, Gotemba, Shizuoka; tel 0550-70-0160

More detail on the dayLeave the house about 09:15, arrive for the 10:00 opening, leave by 12:00, reach Tokyo about 13:30. A later car return can be booked online until 11:00 on Thu 17 Dec, at extra cost. Not Sun 20 Dec.

With the childrenOutlet's service page, read 15 Nov 2026: nursing rooms in all three zones, changing tables in every toilet block, coin strollers, two playgrounds. Open-air site; some paths have no roof.

Costs
  • Shop entry is free. Coin stroller deposit: JPY 100 (≈ SGD 0.81 · ≈ MYR 2.57), given back on return
  • Hill Side playground merry-go-round: JPY 300 (≈ SGD 2.43 · ≈ MYR 7.71) per person

BookingNot needed. Coin strollers cannot be reserved and can all be out at year-end, weekends and holidays.

Age ruleCoin strollers are for ages 4 months to under 48 months. On the merry-go-round, under-5s ride with an adult.

Read 15 Nov 2026: the outlet's shop guide, hours notice and service page, and Sanrio's own store page. Both operators list the shop. Some Hello Kitty items are sold only in one region (Nagoya, Hamamatsu, Mie and Mt Fuji designs exist). No source confirms stock now: ask in the shop.

More Sanrio shops exist and were left out to keep this short: Sanrio NISHIGINZA (Ginza flagship, no lift listed), the Sanrio corner at Takashimaya Nihombashi (5th floor), Sanrio Ueno and Sanrio Shinsaibashi (Osaka), and the districts of Umeda, Harajuku, Ikebukuro, Odaiba and Shibuya.

Not on this route: Sanrio Harmonyland is in Oita, Kyushu, far from this route. Universal Studios Japan lists Hello Kitty areas but is not on our plan.

  • Ise-Shima: no Sanrio shop in Mie, and nothing at Okage Yokocho, VISON, Toba Aquarium or Shima Spain Village.
  • Lake Yamanaka and Mt Fuji: none; the nearest is the Gotemba outlet above.
  • Kansai Airport: nothing before security, and no Hello Kitty train to plan on (the Haruka wrap train cannot be chosen and does not run to Namba).
  • Narita Terminal 1: no Sanrio shop, so buy before the last day.
  • Singapore: only the Jewel store is confirmed. A Sanrio corner at Takashimaya (Orchard Road) and a Chinatown store appear only in third-party listings, and no Sanrio pop-up was found for December.

One park and the Sanrio shops on our way, in trip order.

  • Puroland: yes or no, and Fri 25 or Sat 26 Dec. Decide before the Christmas meals are booked (about Mon 30 Nov). Booking for both days is already open on the official site, and Roy makes the reservation.
  • Nagoya PARCO cafe on Sat 19 Dec (optional): two online bookings of 4 and 3 at the same time. December slots opened on 10 Nov and fill fast.
  • Jewel on Tue 15 Dec (optional): if wanted, book the Changi van for about 17:15 instead of straight after landing (the van is booked by about Wed 9 Dec).
  • Look at the official hours of every shop again about 1 Dec and 15 Dec.

Jewel is joined to Terminal 1, where the Scoot flight lands at 14:35 on Tue 15 Dec. The flights on Wed 16 and Tue 29 Dec leave before it opens.

On Thu 17 Dec the car is collected at 12:00 from the Toyota Namba Terminal Shop, about 14 minutes on foot from the hotel (estimate). Check out first and leave the bags at the front desk: the driver collects the car while the others visit a shop, then everyone meets at the hotel to load.
