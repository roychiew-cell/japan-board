Gotemba Premium Outlets

Large Fuji-view outlet complex with cafés, restaurants and baby facilities.

Route: T/S. Tomei Gotemba IC; Shin-Tomei travellers must choose Gotemba access instead of the direct Shin-Gotemba approach

Extra driving: S: +20 min / +6 km
T: +20 min / +6 km Driving excludes the on-site allowance, parking queues and child-seat handling. Recheck traffic and ramps in navigation on the travel day.
S: Shin-Tomei → Tomei Gotemba IC → outlets → Route 138 / Subashiri (switching included).
T: Gotemba IC → outlets → Route 138 / Subashiri onward.

Time to allow: 60–120 min on site, plus driving, queues and child-seat handling.

Family in December: Convenient facilities but Sunday congestion can delay the final climb to the lake; a lunch-only visit is more manageable. Outdoor shopping lanes/bridges; indoor shops and food courts. Large, hilly multi-zone site. Use lifts and select a small area to avoid a long circuit.

Toilets / baby facilities: Toilets and changing tables listed throughout the complex. Operator lists nursing rooms, hot water/microwaves and private mamaro booths.

Hours (Japan time): Winter seasonal listing: 10:00–19:00. Current generic operator banner says 10:00–20:00 with seasonal variation; exact 20 December hours unverified. Closed: Annual February closure; current operator notice gives 25 February 2027. No regular December closure stated. Exact travel-date operation unverified. Use the winter seasonal schedule provisionally; do not assume the current 20:00 generic closing banner applies in December.

Food budget: Planning budget: meals JPY 1,000–2,500, (≈ SGD 8.10–20.25 · ≈ MYR 25.70–64.25) café JPY 500–1,000 (≈ SGD 4.05–8.10 · ≈ MYR 12.85–25.70); unverified. Admission / parking: Shopping admission free; purchases extra.
