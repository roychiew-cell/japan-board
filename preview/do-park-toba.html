Toba Aquarium

Toba Aquarium (Ise-Shima). Open 09:30 to 17:00 every day, last entry 16:00. Adult JPY 2,800 (≈ SGD 22.68 · ≈ MYR 71.96); children under 3 are free, so JPY 14,000 (≈ SGD 113.40 · ≈ MYR 359.80) for five adults. About 40 minutes by car from Hatago VISON (38 to 43 minutes); Fri 18 Dec is the only free day there, and Port of Nagoya Public Aquarium is already an optional stop on Sat 19 Dec, so choose one aquarium. The Taki VISON expressway gate takes ETC cards only, so the hire car needs one. Parking JPY 1,000 (≈ SGD 8.10 · ≈ MYR 25.70) per car, cash. Own strollers are allowed except inside the show venues. The sea otter Kira died on 5 Nov 2026, so only May is left. Capybaras and how much of the route is indoors are not confirmed: bring coats.
