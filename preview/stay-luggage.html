How to send a bag

Japan's door-to-door luggage delivery. Hand a suitcase to a hotel front desk or convenience store and it is waiting at the next hotel, usually next day.

Yamato counter rates per bag, tax included (checked 28 Sep 2026). Size is length + width + height. An extra-large case (180 cm, 30 kg) is JPY 3,060 (≈ SGD 24.79 · ≈ MYR 78.64) on each sector. Conversions at 1 JPY = 0.0081 SGD = 0.0257 MYR.

Distances are straight-line estimates from the house (683-53 Hirano); the host says shops are about a 20-minute walk.

Easiest: the hotel front desk fills in the slip and hands the bag to Yamato. Or any 7-Eleven or FamilyMart counter. No app or reservation needed.

Usually next day between these cities. Late December is peak season, so allow 2 days.

Next hotel's name, address and phone, the guest name on the booking (Roy Chiew) and the check-in date.

Per bag, at the counter, cash or card. Size is length + width + height.
